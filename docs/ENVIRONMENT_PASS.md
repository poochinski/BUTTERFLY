# Environment pass: felt ground and hand-made construction

The goal: the ground is felt, layered and cut by hand like a professional
crafter's diorama base. Everything built on top of it is cardboard and
construction paper, taped, tied with yarn and glued together.

## Checklist

- [x] **1. Real felt material.** Fibres run in every direction, the dye is
      blotchy, little pills of fluff, a fuzzy sheen at grazing angles, and
      cut edges that show a darker, striated cross-section. Ground sheets
      use coarser fibres (`pattern_scale`).
- [x] **2. Felt tarmac patchwork.** Hand-cut grey felt slabs with wobbly
      edges, in varied tones, over a dark felt underlay that shows in the
      seams. Some slabs have stitched repair patches.
- [x] **3. Felt grass.** Lawns stacked from two or three layers of green
      felt with pinking-shear edges, covered in thousands of fringe-cut felt
      grass tufts and little felt flowers. These line the verges, two
      islands and the fields beyond the fences.
- [x] **4. Mud, ruts and puddles.** Layered brown felt with a wet, glossy
      centre, clods around the edge, muddy tread prints fading as they
      leave, tyre ruts outside the fence, and cellophane puddles.
- [x] **5. Gravel.** Beds of felt pebbles and pom-poms along the fences,
      around the floodlight masts and under the jet bridge legs.
- [x] **6. Imperfections.** Oil stains under the vehicles and engines,
      stray threads on the ground, grass pushing up through cracks
      between slabs.
- [x] **7. Markings and sidewalk.** Taxi lines, the lead-in curve,
      dashes and the stop bar are stitched felt strips. The sidewalk is
      light grey felt paving, partly stitched.
- [x] **8. Masking tape.** Folded around every building corner and over
      the roof edges, strips over wall seams, crossed-tape repairs in
      beige, blue and silver.
- [x] **9. Yarn.** Wrapped round masts, jet bridge legs, fence posts and
      pillars. Tie-down straps knotted over the cargo containers, a power
      cable from the power cart to the airliner, and an extension cable
      along the baggage hall.
- [x] **10. Paper-craft details.** Brass brads pinning the wall panels,
      wall sheets that overlap with a lit paper edge and a cast shadow,
      hot-glue blobs at post bases, and crooked hand-placed crates.
- [x] **11. Verified.** A full match with bots runs with no errors or
      warnings. Screenshots were checked from the ground and from player
      height.
